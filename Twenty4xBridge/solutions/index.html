Our solutions

What do you need to solve?

One platform for Offshore and Main Fleet: maintenance, procurement, crew, safety and fleet insight, from ship to shore.

  • Main Fleet

    One connected view of every vessel. The home dashboard and vessel operations in one place. Shore sees what the vessel records, as it is recorded.

    • I have to ring the superintendent to know what’s happening on a ship.
    Explore Main Fleet

  • Pixel PMS

    Every machine. Its next job. Equipment, stores, maintenance jobs, defects and dry-docking, organised by vessel and ready for class.

    • Is the ship even ready to sail?
    • Dry-dock planning is spreadsheets and emails.
    Explore Pixel PMS

  • E-Chief & Fleet AI

    Catch faults before they stop you. Early warnings and savings opportunities drawn from your own fleet data, with an AI chief engineer on board.

    • We only find out about failures after they happen.
    Explore E-Chief & Fleet AI

  • GoTrac

    From requisition to receipt. Requisition to purchase order, delivery and invoice, with approvals set by value and every step on record.

    • The engineer can’t do the job because the spare isn’t onboard.
    • We order the same part twice, or order what’s already onboard.
    • Approvals are by email, so there’s no audit trail.
    • Vendors are chosen on habit, not performance.
    Explore GoTrac

  • Crewmatic

    The right crew, certified. Crew profiles, rosters, onboarding, travel and a full payroll cycle on one platform.

    • Planning crew changes is a puzzle of ports, flights and visas.
    • Rest hours are filled in to look compliant, not to be accurate.
    • A certificate expires and the vessel gets detained.
    Explore Crewmatic

  • Smart Insights

    The whole fleet at a glance. Live dashboards, rankings and reports that show how every vessel and manager is performing.

    • I can’t compare one vessel against another.
    • A third party manages my ships. I only see what they show me.
    Explore Smart Insights

  • Desktop & mobile

    The link drops. Work continues. Desktop and mobile apps that keep working offline and sync when the link returns.

    • The system breaks when the link drops.
    Explore Desktop & mobile
