How E-Chief & Fleet AI works

Act on a recommendation

  1. Open Fleet AI and filter by entity or category.
  2. Open an opportunity or prediction and read its rationale, definition and source.
  3. Check the underlying records in the relevant module.
  4. Decide and act. The AI supports the decision; your team makes it.
