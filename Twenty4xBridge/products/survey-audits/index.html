How it works

How Survey & Audits works

Conduct an audit

  1. Schedule it in the calendar from a template and assign the auditor.
  2. Answer each question Yes, No or NA, with remarks or evidence where required.
  3. Raise findings with severity, owner and due date, then generate the final report.

Built-in controls

Controls and compliance

  • Findings are graded MNC, NC or OBS.
  • Changing master data never alters a completed audit.
