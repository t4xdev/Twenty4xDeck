How it works

How Corrective Actions works

Raise and close an action

  1. From a finding, incident or defect, assess severity and root cause.
  2. Assign an owner, due date, priority and planned action.
  3. The owner records progress and evidence and submits for verification.
  4. The reviewer closes it or returns it. Reopened actions keep their history.

Built-in controls

Controls and compliance

  • Every action links back to its source record.
  • Closure captures evidence and reviewer verification (configurable).
  • Overdue counts follow the same due-date logic everywhere.
