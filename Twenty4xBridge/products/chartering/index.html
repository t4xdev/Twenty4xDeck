How it works

How Chartering works

Invoice and collect

  1. Generate the invoice for the period.
  2. Check lines, tax and currency, then submit for approval.
  3. Once approved, send it. It cannot be sent or paid before approval.
  4. Record each payment as it arrives.

Built-in controls

Controls and compliance

  • No invoice is sent or paid before approval.
  • Cloning a contract never overwrites the original.
  • Every approval action is recorded.
