How it works

How Crewmatic works

Schedule an assignment

  1. Choose crew, vessel, rank and start and end dates.
  2. Review warnings for overlaps, missing qualifications and certificate validity.
  3. Resolve or confirm. The assignment appears in the roster and the crew member’s timeline.

Run payroll

  1. Import or review attendance for the period.
  2. Prepare the pay run from the salary master and components.
  3. Resolve holds, loans and tax items; apply any adjustments.
  4. Submit through the approval chain, then finalise and download the documents.

Built-in controls

Controls and compliance

  • Pay in INR, USD, AED, PHP, EUR, GBP or SGD, with maintained exchange rates.
  • Statutory packs for India and the UAE included.
  • Closed payroll periods change only through a revision or adjustment.
  • Compensation and personal data are restricted by role and scope.
