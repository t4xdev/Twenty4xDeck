How it works

How Documents works

Upload or renew a document

  1. Upload with metadata and scope. Type and size are checked.
  2. Save. It appears in the library.
  3. As a certificate nears expiry, open it from the expiry list and replace it. Earlier versions are kept.

Built-in controls

Controls and compliance

  • Replacing a document keeps its history and every record that refers to it.
  • Preview and download respect company and vessel scope.
