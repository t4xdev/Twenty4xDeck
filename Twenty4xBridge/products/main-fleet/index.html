How it works

How Main Fleet works

Submit a daily report

  1. Select the vessel and the reporting date.
  2. Enter readings, events and remarks. The form points to any field in error.
  3. Submit. The report is timestamped, attributed to its author and visible to shore.

Complete a voyage

  1. Start up: vessel, origin, departure, engine hours and starting fuel.
  2. Add the voyage crew, then record weather, tide and operational events.
  3. Link a form, job, incident, risk assessment or drill to any event.
  4. Complete the journey and sign off as Master. It moves to Voyage History.

Built-in controls

Controls and compliance

  • Every record carries its vessel, reporting period, author and time.
  • Choice lists such as ports, weather and event types come from controlled master data.
  • Corrections to submitted statutory entries follow an auditable amendment.
