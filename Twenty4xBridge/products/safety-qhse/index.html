    Date, location, severity, parties and evidence, with a PDF report.

  • QHSE alerts

    Issued centrally to vessels, with delivery and acknowledgement tracked.

  • Risk assessment

    Drill library, templates, assignment to vessels, a fill sheet and a report.

How it works

How Safety & QHSE works

Report an incident

  1. Choose event type, date, vessel and location.
  2. Record description, severity and involved parties.
  3. Attach evidence and submit. Raise a corrective action from it.

Issue a QHSE alert

  1. Set title, category and severity and choose target vessels.
  2. Publish. Vessels acknowledge, and you see delivery status.
  3. Review alerts by date, vessel, type and status.

Built-in controls

Controls and compliance

  • Records cannot close while required follow-up actions are outstanding (configurable).
  • Reports filter by date, vessel, type, severity and status, and drill to the source.
