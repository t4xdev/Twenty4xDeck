How it works

How Pixel PMS works

Log and close a defect

  1. Add the defect: vessel, equipment, description, priority and severity.
  2. Attach photos or evidence and assign an owner.
  3. Move it through Open → In Progress → Waiting for Spare → Completed → Closed.
  4. Need a spare? Raise a requisition from the defect so procurement can act.

Plan a docking

  1. Create the schedule entry: vessel, yard, planned dates and scope.
  2. Review conflicts with other docking windows and save.
  3. Record milestones with planned and actual dates.
  4. Log daily yard operations and upload survey documents as they are issued.
