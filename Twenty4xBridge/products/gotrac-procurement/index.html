How it works

How GoTrac works

Buy something

  1. The requester raises a requisition with items, need-by date and justification.
  2. The approver approves, rejects or returns it with comments.
  3. The buyer issues an RFQ and records supplier quotations.
  4. Quotes are compared and the approval chain for that value signs off.
  5. The PO is raised, delivery tracked and the invoice recorded.

Set approval flows

  1. Open Procurement Admin → Flows.
  2. Define value bands per vessel or company: financial or technical, budget or non-budget.
  3. Name the approvers for each band. Records already in flight keep their stages.

Built-in controls

Controls and compliance

  • Approval chains follow value: higher bands add approvers, for example superintendent, then COO, then CFO and CEO.
  • Every decision keeps the approver, time and comment.
  • POs, deliveries and invoices stay linked to their source requisition.
  • Admin changes never alter records already in flight.
